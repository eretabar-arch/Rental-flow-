NEAR DRIVE

Privacy Policy

Pre-launch privacy notice. It will be updated with the final company identity, domain, payment processor and data-retention policy before public launch.

Information we collect

NEAR Drive may collect customer name, email, phone/WhatsApp, country of residence, trip dates, pickup and destination, selected vehicle, optional flight information and delivery details necessary to process a reservation.

How we use it

Information is used to prepare and manage rental reservations, communicate with customers, coordinate supplier confirmation, provide support and maintain transaction records.

Suppliers

Reservation information required to fulfill the rental may be shared with the selected rental company or service provider.

Security

Administrative access and service credentials should remain server-side. Payment card information will be handled by the selected payment processor rather than stored directly by NEAR Drive.

Your choices

Customers may contact NEAR Drive regarding their reservation information and applicable access, correction or deletion requests.
