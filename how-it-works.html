HOW IT WORKS

One search. The right car. The real price.

NEAR Drive is designed for Costa Rica. We analyze your trip, rank vehicles for the route, normalize the full rental cost and prepare a prepaid reservation so pickup is simple.

01 · SEARCH

Tell us where you are going

Enter your pickup point, destination and dates. NEAR uses the route context before ranking vehicles.

02 · NEAR AI

Your route changes the recommendation

Route difficulty, road suitability, vehicle capability, rental conditions and price all influence the Match Score.

03 · TRUE PRICE™

See the complete rental cost

NEAR separates rental, mandatory coverage, taxes, fees and refundable security deposit so you know what is actually being paid.

04 · PREPAID PICKUP

NEAR handles the supplier

You pay NEAR for the rental. NEAR confirms and prepays the rental company. At pickup, you sign the supplier contract and provide the refundable security deposit directly to the rental company.
